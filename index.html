Draw your own fighter

Aholo Lux3D turns your doodle into a 3D toy with a skeleton and ten moves, live. About 3 minutes.

  • Draw one character: a head, a body, arms out, two legs.
  • Colour it in. Big simple shapes work best.
  • It can be anything: a robot, a carrot, your cat.
  • Drew it on paper? Tap Use a photo.
